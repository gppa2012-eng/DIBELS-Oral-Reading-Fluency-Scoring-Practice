Scoring Oral Reading Fluency

DIBELS 8th Edition ORF Benchmark 2.Middle · "Pay Phones"

Examiner script "Please read this (point to passage) out loud. If you get stuck, I will tell you the word, so you can keep reading. When I say 'Stop' I may ask you to tell me about what you read, so do your best reading. Start here (point to first word of first paragraph of passage). Ready? Begin."
Reminders Start timer: when the student says the first word.
Prompts: if the student hesitates, wait 3 seconds, give the correct word, and mark the missed word as incorrect.
Discontinue: if the student does not get any words correct within the first line, discontinue ORF.

Score the passage

As the student reads, click a word to slash it as an error. Use the bracket tool to mark the last word read when the timer ends. Self-corrections are not errors.
